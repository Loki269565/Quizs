# PART A — SINGLE UNIFIED GENERAL AWARENESS MASTER SYLLABUS
### (Merges RRB Technician Grade‑I Signal + SSC CGL Tier‑1 + DSSSB — all overlapping topics combined into ONE study list so you prepare once for all three)

Legend: 🔥 Asked in all 3 exams (top priority) | ⭐ Asked in 2 of 3 exams | ➡️ Asked in 1 exam / lower frequency

## 1. History
- 🔥 Modern Indian History — Freedom Struggle, 1857 Revolt, national movements, leaders
- ⭐ Ancient India — Indus Valley, Vedic period, Mauryas, Guptas
- ⭐ Medieval India — Delhi Sultanate, Mughal Empire
- ➡️ World History — major revolutions, World Wars

## 2. Polity & Constitution
- 🔥 Fundamental Rights & Duties, Preamble, DPSP
- 🔥 Parliament (Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha), law-making process
- ⭐ President, PM, Governor, Judiciary structure
- ⭐ Constitutional Amendments, Panchayati Raj
- ➡️ Constitutional bodies — Election Commission, CAG, UPSC

## 3. Geography
- 🔥 Indian Physical Geography — rivers, mountains, plateaus
- ⭐ States & Capitals, climate & monsoon
- ➡️ World Geography — major physical features, continents/oceans

## 4. Economy
- 🔥 RBI & Banking — functions, monetary policy tools, repo rate
- ⭐ Basic economic concepts — GDP/GNP, Five-Year Plans, NITI Aayog
- ➡️ Union Budget highlights, Economic Survey

## 5. Science (Static + Everyday)
- 🔥 Physics, Chemistry, Biology — basic conceptual/everyday science
- ⭐ Scientific instruments, inventions & discoveries
- ⭐ Recent developments — ISRO missions, DRDO, biotech, space
- ➡️ Environment & ecology

## 6. Static GK (Fact-based, high-frequency one-liners)
- 🔥 Important Days & Dates
- 🔥 Awards & Honours (Padma awards, Nobel, Bharat Ratna, sports awards)
- 🔥 Books & Authors
- 🔥 Famous Personalities
- ⭐ Countries & Currencies, National symbols
- ⭐ Art & Culture — dance forms, festivals, monuments, UNESCO heritage sites

## 7. Sports
- 🔥 Major tournaments, winners, records
- ⭐ Sports awards, sporting bodies (BCCI, IOC, etc.)

## 8. Current Affairs (Last 8–12 months — highest ROI across ALL THREE exams)
- 🔥 National & international news
- 🔥 Government schemes & policies
- 🔥 Awards, appointments, obituaries
- 🔥 Sports events & outcomes
- ⭐ Defence exercises, summits, new legislation
- ⭐ Budget & Economic Survey highlights
- ➡️ Railway-specific current affairs (new trains, Kavach, Vande Bharat) — extra for RRB
- ➡️ National/International Organisations & Institutions — extra for DSSSB

## 9. Miscellaneous / Exam-specific extras
- ➡️ Basics of Computers & Applications (RRB Technician Grade‑I only — hardware, information processing cycle)
- ➡️ Physics/Electronics/Electrical fundamentals (RRB Technician Grade‑I, under Basic Science & Engineering — separate from GA but overlaps with static science)
